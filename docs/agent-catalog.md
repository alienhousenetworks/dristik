# Dristik 2.0: 30-Agent Master SEO Engine Catalog

## Layer 1: Scope, Evidence Governance & Policy
- **Agent 01: Audit Orchestrator**: Manages workflow execution across all stages, builds the execution DAG, and records state transitions.
- **Agent 02: Evidence & Data Integrity Guard**: Verifies data provenance, snapshot immutability, and compliance with evidence policy.

## Layer 2: Crawling & Discovery
- **Agent 03: Crawling & Discovery Engineer**: Coordinates native HTTP, Playwright headless browser, and Firecrawl scrapers.
- **Agent 04: Crawlability & Indexation Agent**: Audits robots.txt, sitemaps, HTTP status codes, and canonical declarations.

## Layer 3: Technical Architecture & Performance
- **Agent 05: Technical Architecture & URL Integrity**: Evaluates URL hierarchy, redirect chains, HTTPS/HSTS, and clean parameter handling.
- **Agent 06: JavaScript & Client-Side Rendering**: Compares raw HTML against rendered DOM to detect hydration and content injection gaps.
- **Agent 07: Performance & Core Web Vitals**: Analyzes LCP, INP, CLS, TTFB, and server response metrics.
- **Agent 08: Structured Data & Knowledge Graph**: Validates Schema.org JSON-LD, microdata, entity graph connectivity, and syntax.

## Layer 4: Content Quality & Semantic Relevance
- **Agent 09: Metadata & SERP Snippet Optimization**: Analyzes title tags, meta descriptions, OpenGraph, and Twitter cards.
- **Agent 10: Content Depth & Information Gain**: Evaluates topical completeness, original insights, fluff ratio, and entity coverage.
- **Agent 11: Keyword Intent & Query Mapping**: Maps target queries to URL pages, detecting intent mismatch and cannibalization.
- **Agent 12: Internal Linking & PageRank Flow**: Analyzes internal link equity distribution, anchor text diversity, and orphan pages.
- **Agent 13: Readability & Plain English**: Assesses accessibility, Flesch-Kincaid grade level, and clarity for non-technical audiences.
- **Agent 14: Media, Asset & Accessibility**: Audits image optimization, alt text, responsive image tags, and audio/video transcripts.

## Layer 5: Authority & Market Competition
- **Agent 15: Backlink Quality & Toxic Link Profile**: Analyzes external referring domains, authority metrics, anchor distributions, and disavow risks.
- **Agent 16: Competitive Gap & SERP Overlap**: Compares keyword footprint and domain authority against market rivals.
- **Agent 17: Local SEO & Multi-Location**: Audits Google Business Profile consistency, NAP data, and local citations.
- **Agent 18: International & Multi-Regional SEO**: Evaluates hreflang implementations, regional geo-targeting, and currency/language tags.
- **Agent 19: Brand Sentiment & Knowledge Entity**: Tracks brand entity salience, Wikipedia/Wikidata presence, and sentiment signals.

## Layer 6: Generative Engine Optimization (GEO) & AEO
- **Agent 20: AI Search Citation & Engine Readiness**: Assesses visibility across ChatGPT Search, Perplexity, Gemini, and Claude.
- **Agent 21: Answer Engine Optimization (AEO)**: Analyzes conversational Q&A readiness, featured snippet extraction, and direct answers.
- **Agent 22: RAG Chunking & Synthesizer Indexing**: Evaluates site parseability for retrieval-augmented generation chunking.

## Layer 7: Business Outcomes, CRO & Risk
- **Agent 23: Conversion Rate Optimization (CRO)**: Audits visual call-to-actions, form UX, value propositions, and friction points.
- **Agent 24: Search Console & Analytics Attribution**: Correlates audit findings with actual impressions, clicks, and ranking shifts.
- **Agent 25: Lifecycle Revenue & ROI Impact**: Quantifies financial impact and projected revenue upside of proposed fixes.
- **Agent 26: Business Risk & Regulatory Compliance**: Evaluates copyright risks, privacy policy compliance, GDPR, and algorithmic penalty risks.

## Layer 8: Synthesis, Remediation & Delivery
- **Agent 27: Finding Deduplication & Conflict Resolver**: Merges overlapping observations into unified, non-redundant findings.
- **Agent 28: Remediation Planner & Patch Generator**: Generates actionable code diffs, server configs, and developer patch packs.
- **Agent 29: Verification & Rollback Engine**: Re-tests applied patches against staging environments and provides rollback recipes.
- **Agent 30: Executive Book Synthesizer & PDF Generator**: Compiles publication-grade, multi-chapter SEO Audit Book in PDF and HTML.
