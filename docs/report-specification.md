# Dristik 2.0 Multi-Chapter Report Specification

## Report Formats
- **Interactive Web Report**: Responsive React workspace with live filtering, evidence drawer, and patch approvals.
- **Publication-Grade PDF Book**: Multi-chapter bound report compiled via WeasyPrint/Headless Chrome, featuring:
  - Chapter 01: Executive Summary & Health Scorecards
  - Chapter 02: Crawlability & Technical Foundation
  - Chapter 03: Architecture, URLs & Performance
  - Chapter 04: Content Quality, Intent & Keywords
  - Chapter 05: Authority & Competitive Landscape
  - Chapter 06: Generative Engine Optimization (GEO & AEO)
  - Chapter 07: Conversion UX & Business Risk
  - Chapter 08: Prioritized Remediation Roadmap & Developer Patch Pack
