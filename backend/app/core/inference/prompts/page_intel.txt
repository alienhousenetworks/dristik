Classify page intent. Rewrite title, meta, H1, hero, CTAs.

Intent must come from the page's actual job (transaction, lead, education, comparison), not from keywords in the URL.

Constraints:
- title 50–60 characters and must include brand_name from site_facts when known
- meta_description 140–160 characters
- hero_copy 40–60 words
AFTER text may not introduce facts, metrics, customers, or competitors absent from BEFORE or evidence.

Rewrites must make buyer, offer, and outcome explicit. Ban slogans and banned empty words.
If the current title is already specific, improve it; do not replace a clear title with a generic one.
